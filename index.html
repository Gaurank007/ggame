RULES & CONTROLS

Move, fight, survive

  • Move with WASD or arrow keys; aim with the mouse; hold click or Space to fire.
  • Shift dashes for 0.18 seconds and then recharges for 1.5 seconds.
  • Gain skill points over time and press Q to activate your class skill.
  • The safe zone shrinks; being outside it drains health. Health regenerates after 25 seconds without damage.
  • Press Tab to hold the scoreboard. Kills, assists and objectives award score.

Choose your ship

  • Specter: fast, fragile, rapid-fire shots; binds one enemy.
  • Bulwark: slow and tough, fires a close-range spread; charges a nuclear blast.
  • Aegis: durable support, locks enemies with its laser; heals nearby allies or itself.
  • Eclipse: balanced hex volleys, target seeking and a nanobot weakness effect.

Match formats

  • Free for all: each pilot scores independently.
  • Team battle: cyan and magenta teams; friendly fire is disabled.
  • Last ship / Last team: no respawns after elimination.
  • King of the hill: hold the moving zone to earn team points.
  • Timed matches end at the timer; score races end at the target. Team sizes are 2v2, 3v3 or 4v4.

Touch controls

  • Drag the left stick to move and the right stick to aim.
  • Aiming with the right stick fires automatically by default; change this in Settings.
  • Use FIRE when auto-fire is off. DASH and SKILL are separate buttons.
  • Use the fullscreen button for more play area. Rotate to landscape for the clearest view.
